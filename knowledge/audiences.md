# Audiences

Four audiences use Petit Meme. Each one keeps everything the audience before it has.

## Visitor

Anyone, with no account. A Visitor browses the whole library and watches every Meme. A Visitor also downloads or shares the video with the watermark, and searches the library. The consent banner is the only thing that interrupts them.

Some features need an account: bookmarks, the Studio, the AI search, proposals. When a Visitor reaches for one, Petit Meme asks them to sign in. Saying no leaves them where they were.

## User

A Visitor with an account. Sign-up works with an email address, with X or with Discord. An email account has to confirm its address: a reminder goes out, and an account that never confirms is deleted.

On top of what a Visitor has, a User:

- bookmarks Memes, makes captioned versions in the Studio, and searches with the AI search, each up to a cap
- proposes Memes to the Creator, with a limit on the proposals waiting for a decision
- downloads their personal data, and deletes their account

A User who is paying for Premium cancels the subscription before deleting the account. Inactive accounts are anonymised after several years.

## Premium

A User with Premium access. Premium comes from a paid subscription, or as a gift from the Creator. Premium lifts the caps on bookmarks, the Studio and the AI search, and adds:

- the video without the watermark
- the sound of every Meme
- no more invitations to subscribe

See [the offer](offer.md) for how Premium starts, ends and gets billed.

## Creator

The person who runs Petit Meme, and the only administrator. The Creator counts as Premium, and works in the administration, in French only.

For the library, the Creator:

- imports Memes from an X link or a file, fills their record with AI Assist, and publishes them
- previews a Meme on the public site before it goes live
- accepts or rejects the Memes Users propose

For the people, the Creator:

- follows activity, subscriptions and trends on a dashboard, and receives a weekly recap by email
- opens each User's record: account, Premium state, subscription and invoice history, activity, moderation
- gives or takes back lifetime Premium, bans and deletes accounts

The Creator's own views stay out of the public view counters.
