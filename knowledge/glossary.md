# Glossary

One word, one meaning, across the product, the administration and these documents.

## People

- **Visitor**: anyone on the site, with no account.
- **User**: a Visitor with an account.
- **Premium**: a User with Premium access, paid or offered.
- **Creator**: the person who runs Petit Meme, its only administrator.

## The library

- **Meme**: one short video in the library, with its record.
- **Record**: the title, description, keywords, categories, content language and translations of a Meme.
- **Category**: a group of Memes on one theme.
- **Content language**: French, English or universal. It decides on which site a Meme shows.
- **Universal Meme**: a Meme that carries no language, understood by French and English speakers alike.
- **Status**: where a Meme stands. Pending, Published or Archived.
- **Pending**: imported, not live yet, visible to the Creator only.
- **Published**: live, for everyone.
- **Archived**: taken off the site.
- **Proposal**: a Meme a User suggests with a link. The Creator accepts it, and it becomes a Meme, or rejects it.
- **Preview** (of a Meme): the public page of a Pending Meme, open to the Creator only.
- **Thumbnail**: the still image of a Meme. **Animated preview**: the short loop shown on hover. Both are public.
- **Original**: the source video file. Only Petit Meme's server reaches it.

## What people do

- **View**: one opening of a Meme's page.
- **Export**: taking a Meme away, as a download or through the share sheet.
- **Download** and **share**: the two kinds of export. Copying a Meme's link counts as a share.
- **Video**: the exported video file. **Sound**: the exported audio file, Premium only.
- **Watermark**: the mark on the free video. Premium gets the video without it.
- **Bookmark**: a Meme a User saved to find again.
- **Studio**: where a User adds a caption to a Meme and exports the result.
- **Caption**: the text a User adds in the Studio. Memes themselves carry no subtitles.
- **Generation**: one captioned video made in the Studio.
- **AI search**: a search from a description in plain words.
- **Reels**: the phone feed that chains Memes one after the other.
- **Embed**: a Meme played on another site through Petit Meme's player page.

## The offer

- **Free**: the plan without payment. The whole library, with caps.
- **Cap**: the limit Free puts on bookmarks, generations and AI searches.
- **Invitation** (to subscribe): the dialog shown when a free person reaches for a Premium feature.
- **Reminder**: the occasional prompt that suggests Premium, once, without blocking.
- **Subscription states**: Active, Ending, Payment failing, Ended, Offered. See [the offer](offer.md).
- **Offered Premium**: lifetime Premium the Creator gives, with no payment.
- **Paid months**: the months a User really paid for.

## The Creator's tools

- **Administration**: the Creator's side of the site, in French.
- **Import**: creating a Meme from an X link or a file.
- **AI Assist**: the tool that watches a Meme and proposes its record.
- **Publish**: building a Meme's exported files and putting it live.
- **Regenerate**: rebuilding the exported files of a published Meme.
- **Activity**: the record of what people did, shown in the administration.
- **Weekly recap**: the Sunday email with the week's figures.
