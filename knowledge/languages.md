# Languages

Petit Meme speaks French and English. The audience and the Memes are French first, so French is the base language. English opens the library to the rest of the world, and English pages live under their own path.

## One feature, two languages

A feature ships in both languages at once, or it ships half done. That covers every screen, every email to a User, the page titles and descriptions that search engines read, and the legal pages. The legal pages change in both languages together, with their date.

Only the administration and the emails the Creator receives stay in French, because the Creator is their only reader.

## A Meme's language

Each Meme has a content language:

- **French**: understood by French speakers. It needs its French record.
- **English**: understood by English speakers. It needs its English record.
- **Universal**: understood by anyone, French or English speaker, because it carries no language. It needs both records, and the translation is automatic.

The content language decides where a Meme shows. The French site shows French, English and universal Memes, because a French speaker often gets an English meme. The English site shows English and universal Memes only, because the reverse rarely holds. A Visitor can narrow the library to one language.
