# The life of a Meme

A Meme is one short video in the library, with a title, a description, keywords, categories and a content language. It goes through four steps: discovery, import, record, publication.

## Discovery

The Creator scrolls X on a phone and saves the posts worth keeping. Saved posts also hold things that are not memes, so nothing gets imported blindly: the Creator picks each one.

Users can also propose a Meme with a link. The Creator accepts or rejects each proposal, and a reminder email lists the ones waiting.

## Import

The Creator pastes an X link or uploads a file in the administration. The Meme is created at once, in the Pending status, with a default title. The video goes to the video platform, which encodes it and reports back when it is ready.

## Record

Writing the record by hand is what slowed the library down: without new records, nothing new reaches the site. AI Assist now watches the video, listens to its sound, and proposes the title, the description, the keywords and the translations. The tweet text plays no part: people rarely describe the video in it.

A human still checks every record before it goes live. The page saves itself as the Creator types, so nothing gets lost.

## Publication

Publishing builds the two files people take away: the video with the watermark, and the sound. A Meme needs both to go live. Once published, it appears on the site, in search, in its categories, and in what Google reads to show it in video results.

Before publishing, the Creator can open the Meme's public page as a preview, with a banner saying it is not live yet, and already download or share it. Everyone else gets a "not found" page, and Google never sees it.

## Statuses

- **Pending**: imported, not live yet. Only the Creator sees it.
- **Published**: live, for everyone.
- **Archived**: taken off the site, for everyone, the Creator included.

A Meme can also carry a Rejected status, but the Creator does not use it. A refused proposal stays a proposal and never becomes a Meme.

## Where it goes

The aim is for the Creator to add a Meme and walk away. AI fills the record in the background, the Creator gets a digest of the Memes ready to check, and validates them. The end goal is publication by default: the AI publishes, and the Creator corrects afterwards.

## What a Meme never has

Subtitles. The text a User adds in the Studio is a caption on their own copy, not part of the Meme.
