# The product

Petit Meme is a library of video memes. Anyone can open it, watch a Meme, download it or share it, without an account.

It started from the Creator's own need: find a video meme fast and send it to friends.

## What it does

- **Watch.** The home page opens on a video that plays. Each Meme has its own page, and on a phone the Reels chain the Memes one after the other.
- **Find.** A search, categories, the trending Memes, related Memes and a random Meme. Signed-in Users also get an AI search that understands a description in plain words.
- **Take away.** Every Meme exports as a video, by download or through the phone's share sheet. Premium also exports its sound.
- **Make your own.** The Studio adds a caption to a Meme and exports the result.
- **Keep.** Signed-in Users bookmark the Memes they want to find again.
- **Install.** Petit Meme installs as an app on the phone's home screen, with no app store. It opens full screen, and a lost connection shows a page that waits for the network instead of the browser's error.

## Who runs it

One person, the Creator, finds the Memes, publishes them and builds the site. Almost every Meme comes from X (Twitter), where the Creator saves the ones worth keeping. Users can also propose a Meme, and the Creator decides whether it joins the library, but that path stays secondary.

## Who it is for

Two groups, with the same weight:

- people who send a meme in a conversation or a group
- content creators who drop a meme into their own video

Both need the meme as a file. The terms of use let them reuse what they download, resale included, because Petit Meme does not own the videos.

## How it makes money

A Premium subscription, monthly or yearly. Premium lifts the caps and adds comfort: the video without the watermark, the sound, unlimited Studio and AI search. Nothing in the library sits behind it. See [the offer](offer.md).

## Where it stands

The site is live, with real accounts, real subscriptions and real invoices. People arrive, sign up and subscribe. Most of the traffic comes from France. Google video search is one of the ways people find a Meme.
